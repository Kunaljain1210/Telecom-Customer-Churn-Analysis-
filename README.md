📉 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI project analyzing churn behavior across 7,000+ telecom customers — uncovering why customers leave, who is most at risk, and what actions actually reduce churn.

🎯 Project Overview

Customer churn was costing this telecom business $3.68M in lost revenue (17.24% of total revenue) at a 26.54% churn rate — well above the ~20% industry average.

This project breaks the problem down across three connected dashboards to answer:

What is churn costing us, and where is it concentrated?
Who are the customers most likely to churn?
Why are they leaving, and what can we do about it?
📊 Dashboard Pages
1️⃣ Churn Overview

High-level KPIs and revenue impact:

1,550 customers analyzed in this view | 166 churned | 10.71% churn rate
Revenue lost by churn category — competitor offers account for $0.38M alone
Churn rate by internet service type: Fiber Optic (16.70%) vs. No Internet (2.46%)
Top 7 reported reasons customers gave for leaving
2️⃣ Customer Demographics & Risk Analysis

Segmentation to identify who is at risk:

Average customer: 46.5 years old, 32.4 months tenure, $3.03K average revenue
Customers aged 60+ churn at 35.41% — the highest-risk and highest-value segment ($71.77 avg monthly charge)
Geographic distribution across 1,106 cities
Family/dependent status as a churn indicator (no-dependent households are highest risk)
3️⃣ Retention Drivers & Action Plan

Turning insights into strategy:

Highest churn drivers: Month-to-Month contracts, Fiber Optic, Mailed Check payments
Online Security reduces churn by 27.16%; Premium Tech Support reduces it by 26.47%
Device Protection reduces churn by 16.63%
A concrete 6-point retention action plan (contract conversion, service bundling, digital payment migration, high-risk targeting)
🔍 Key Insights
Insight	Impact

Clone this repo

💡 Recommended Actions (from the dashboard)
Convert Month-to-Month customers to annual contracts via discount incentives
Improve Fiber Optic service quality to address the top complaint driver
Migrate Mailed Check users to AutoPay/Credit Card
Bundle Online Security, Tech Support, and Device Protection
Proactively target high-risk customers with early retention campaigns
Monitor churn KPIs continuously to measure campaign effectiveness
